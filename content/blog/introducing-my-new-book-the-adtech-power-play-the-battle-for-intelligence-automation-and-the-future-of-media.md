A New Perspective on the Future of Advertising: Introducing The AdTech Power Play

The advertising industry is changing faster than ever. Technology, data, artificial intelligence and automation are reshaping how media is planned, bought, delivered and measured. But beyond all the innovation, one question keeps coming back: Who will shape the future of advertising, and what will it take to stay ahead?

Over time, through my work, research and conversations across the AdTech and MarTech industry, I have found myself exploring this question from different perspectives. That journey has now taken a new form with the publication of my book, The AdTech Power Play: The Battle for Intelligence, Automation and the Future of Media.

This book brings together my thoughts, industry observations and research on the changing advertising ecosystem, covering AdTech, MarTech, programmatic advertising, OOH, DOOH, data, measurement and the growing role of AI.

I wanted to look beyond the technology itself and explore the bigger picture: how platforms are evolving, where the real competition lies, how intelligence is becoming a differentiator, and why automation and AI could fundamentally change the way the advertising industry operates.

For me, this book is more than a collection of industry insights. It is an opportunity to put forward ideas, question existing approaches and encourage conversations about what comes next.

I also believe that the future of this industry shouldn’t be discussed from just one perspective. Advertisers, agencies, media owners, technology providers, product leaders and industry professionals all have something valuable to contribute.

I would genuinely love to hear your perspective.

If you work in AdTech, MarTech, media, advertising or technology, I invite you to explore The AdTech Power Play. Read it, challenge the ideas, share your feedback and let me know what resonates with you.

If there is a topic you’d like to discuss further, or if you have a different point of view, I’d be happy to connect and exchange thoughts.

Because the future of advertising is not something we should simply watch unfold. It’s something we should actively question, understand and help shape.

Let’s start a conversation.

— Suraj Prakash
Author | Product Leader | AdTech & MarTech Researcher
