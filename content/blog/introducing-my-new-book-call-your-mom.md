There’s one phone call most of us keep moving to tomorrow.

We’ll call after this meeting. After the deadline. Once things settle down. And somewhere, a mother looks at her phone and tells herself we must be busy.

I wrote *Call Your Mom* about that call. About the little things we take for granted: the “Have you eaten?”, the voice that always sounds happy to hear from us, the conversations we keep saving for later, and the thank-you we never quite say out loud.

It isn’t here to make anyone feel guilty. It’s a gentle reminder for anyone who has ever thought, “I should call her,” and then didn’t.

So here’s one small request. Before the book, before anything else today, pick up your phone and call your mom. The book can wait a few minutes.
